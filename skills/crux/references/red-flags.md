# Red flags (check in every run)

Report only flags you can point to with a location. Each goes into "RIZIKA A MEZERY"
as `⚠ <what> (§x / s. y)`.

## Typical AI defects

- **Made-up or unverifiable sources**: URLs that do not exist, papers or reports you
  cannot find, quotes with no origin, oddly precise statistics with no source.
- **Confidence without support**: "studies show", "it is widely known", "clearly"
  with nothing behind it.
- **Filler**: generic phrases that apply to any topic ("zvažte další kroky",
  "klíčové je zaměřit se na kvalitu"), repetition, the same point reworded.
- **Template structure with no content**: every section looks the same, lists of
  advantages and disadvantages that are not specific to this case.
- **False balance or hedging** that avoids a recommendation the brief asked for.
- **Stale or wrong-context facts**: old prices, versions or laws, another country's
  rules.

## Gaps and inconsistencies

- Numbers that do not add up, or differ between sections.
- A conclusion that does not follow from the body, or contradicts it.
- Missing what a decision needs: costs, risks, alternatives, a timeline, an owner,
  how success is measured.
- Unstated assumptions the conclusion depends on.
- Scope drift: answering a different or easier question than the brief asked.
